# RV Portfolio

Marketing/portfolio site for Romana Vítková, a women's fitness coach. Single-page site, CZ-only for now.
_Note_: EN translations exist in code but are not user-reachable (no language switcher, no browser-language detection). Kept for future reuse.

## Language

**Hybrid coaching**:
Combination of in-person 1:1 training plus ongoing online support (plan + check-ins). The most popular offering.
_Avoid_: Hybrid spolupráce (old term, same concept)

**Online coaching**:
Fully remote coaching — training plan, consultations, and support delivered without in-person sessions.

**Personal coaching**:
In-person-only training, no online/remote component. Distinct third offering, separate from Hybrid.
_Avoid_: Osobní trénink, in-person coaching (as a standalone term — use "Personal coaching")
